# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be welcoming and kind to people of every background and experience level.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Give and take feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, insults, sexual language or imagery, trolling, or publishing anyone's private information.
- Assume good intent, and help newcomers find their way.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior worries you, contact the maintainers privately (see the repository's profile or [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for the private reporting channel). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior breaks this code.
